# Lenses

A lens is a framework, model or method used to understand digital leadership. Lenses are applied in [synthesis](../synthesis/), and are also objects of study with a recorded standing. Template: [lens.md](../templates/lens.md).

| Kind | Folder | Asks |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | [domain/](domain/) | What do leaders need? |
| Systems | [systems/](systems/) | How is the system structured and bounded? |
| Discourse | [discourse/](discourse/) | How does the language make its claims? |
